# Build Log — Darknode — Desktop App

> **Why this file exists.** In fall 2029 you will write a college essay. The single
> best material for it is *the story of how you built this* — the decisions, the bugs,
> the moments something worked. You will **not** remember those details in three years.
> Git history shows *what* changed; this file records *why*, and how it felt. Two
> minutes per entry now saves the whole story later.
>
> **How to use it.** Add an entry whenever you do something worth remembering — you
> don't need to log every day. Keep it in your own words. Newest entry on top.
> Be honest about what you didn't understand yet; that's the most useful part later.

---

## Entry template — copy this block for each entry

```
### YYYY-MM-DD — <one-line title of what happened>

**What I set out to do:**
<the goal in one or two sentences>

**What I actually did / what I decided:**
<the real work. If you chose between two approaches, say which and WHY.>

**What broke, and how I figured it out:**
<the bug, the wrong assumption, the thing that took hours. This is the gold.>

**What I learned (in my own words):**
<one thing you understand now that you didn't this morning>

**How much was me vs. AI help:**
<honest note — e.g. "AI wrote the first draft of X, I redesigned Y, I debugged Z myself".
This matters: colleges care that YOU understand it. Being able to say exactly what you
did is a strength, not a weakness.>
```
